# Dusty's World Border v1.3.6

# **Patch Notes** -  3/29/25 @ 12:16 AM PT

---

## **Fixed**  
- **Teleportation During Loading Screens:** No more crashes or freezes when transferring dimensions (Nether/End). The script now detects loading screens and delays teleportation until safe.

- **Ground Teleport Bug:** Players are now placed **above ground** using a top-to-bottom search for the highest block.  

- **Multiple Teleports:** Fixed repeated teleportation (up to 5 times) when returning inside the border—now happens only once.  

---  

## **Improved**  
- **Teleportation Delay:** Increased to **5 seconds** after exiting the border, improving stability on low-end PCs and mobile.  

- **Re-Entry Detection:** If you return within **5 seconds**, teleportation is canceled, preventing unnecessary warping.  

---  

## **Added**  
- **Dimension-Specific Logic:** Seamless transitions between **Overworld, Nether, and End**—no more crashes or freezes.
